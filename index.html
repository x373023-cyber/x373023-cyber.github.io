03  Info

Rao Birui (Sue) studies Visual Communication Design at Chongqing University. Her projects start from culture and ecology and end in things people can watch, hold, wear or step into.

Household deities rebuilt as animated bodies, a shrine that answers an offering card, a purifier for herders’ river water, a belt that feels the trunk during a forehand. She builds most of it herself: modelling and rendering, interaction logic, electronics and interfaces.
